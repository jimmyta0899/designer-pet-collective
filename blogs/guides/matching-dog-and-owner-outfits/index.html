Matching Dog and Owner Outfits: How to Do It Without Looking Silly

Matching outfits can be charming or cringe. The difference is restraint. Here's how to pull off the look — and why it makes such good photos.

Start with one shared element

The easiest version is a single matching piece — a tee with the same print on both human and pet. You don't need to coordinate the whole outfit. Our Matching Human-Pet Tee does the work in one item.

Keep the rest quiet

Let the matching piece be the statement. Neutral trousers and simple shoes on you; nothing else on the dog. Restraint is what makes it read as style, not costume.

Fit comes first

A poorly fitting pet tee undermines the whole look. Measure chest, neck and back, and size up between sizes. A comfortable dog photographs better, too.

When to wear it

Matching sets shine at home, on walks and in photos — they're a social object as much as clothing. Keep it occasional and it stays special.
