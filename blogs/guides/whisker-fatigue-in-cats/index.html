Whisker Fatigue in Cats: Signs and the Right Bowl

If your cat pulls food out of the bowl to eat it off the floor, the bowl might be the problem. Here's what whisker fatigue looks like.

What whisker fatigue is

A cat's whiskers are sensitive. When a deep bowl presses them against the sides, some cats become stressed at mealtime — a phenomenon commonly called whisker fatigue.

Signs to watch

Pawing food out onto the floor, hesitating at the bowl, eating only from the edges or pulling back after a bite can all point to discomfort with the bowl.

The right shape

Wide and shallow is the answer. Our Giant Rock bowl is low-profile so whiskers never touch the sides, while the Lake plate adds gentle ripples for cats that also eat too fast.

Try it gradually

Offer the new shallow bowl alongside the old one. Most cats take to it quickly once the pressure on their whiskers is gone.
