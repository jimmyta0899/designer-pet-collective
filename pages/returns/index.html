Returns

We want you to love what arrives. Here's how we handle the practical realities of cross-border returns.

30-day window

Contact us within 30 days of delivery if something isn't right. We'll review each case and work toward a fair outcome.

Cross-border reality

Returning an item to China is costly and slow. For defective or incorrect items, we'll arrange a replacement or refund without asking you to ship back where impractical.

Apparel sizing

For clothing, please use the Measure Guide before ordering and size up between sizes. Most sizing issues come from measurement, not quality, and we'll help you find the right fit.

How to start

Email us with your order number and a description (photos help). We reply within one business day.
