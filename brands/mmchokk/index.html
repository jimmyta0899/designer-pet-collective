The story

MMCHOKK treats pets the way a fashion house treats a muse. Knitwear, outerwear, European crystal and the three coastal colours of Cinque Terre — each piece built to be seen and photographed.

The through-line is the human-pet connection: matching tees, matching collars and charms, the couple's-shirt logic applied to the person and their dog.

A new brand with a clear point of view. Now in the US for the first time.
