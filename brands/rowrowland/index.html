The story

Designer Wang Yuan looked at the land — lakes, volcanoes, giant rock, wind erosion — and asked what a dog bowl could look like if it belonged in a home, not under a counter.

The result is the Landform Series: food-grade ceramic pieces shaped like terrain, all snapping onto one magnetic base. One bowl or four. A slow feeder or a wide, whisker-safe plate. Reconfigure it whenever you like.

The brand has been recognised by Pentawards, and its landform bowls are the bestsellers in its own right. This is their first collection in the US.
